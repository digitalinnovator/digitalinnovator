## About me

I'm an AI engineer and AI architect with **12+ years** of experience taking AI from idea to production. I've led engineering teams at **Giisty** and **Hyves.co** in Dubai, and today I work with startups and enterprises worldwide as a freelance AI engineer and consultant.

- 📍 Based in Dubai, UAE — working remotely with clients across the US, Europe and the Middle East
